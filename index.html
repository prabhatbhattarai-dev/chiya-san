Our Story

More than a café — a celebration of tea.

Chiya SAN is a vibrant cultural hub in Lalitpur, dedicated to Nepal's beloved tea tradition. Located in the historic Chakupat area, we celebrate "Chiya" not just as a beverage, but as a social ritual that binds communities together.

The atmosphere is lively and inviting — often featuring live music, poetry readings, and cultural performances that reflect the rich heritage of the Kathmandu Valley.
